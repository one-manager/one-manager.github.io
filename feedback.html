YOUR FEEDBACK

Help me improve MARU Apps. Report a bug, request a feature, or just share your thoughts — every message reaches me directly.

Send Feedback

Your feedback helps me improve MARU apps every single day. Whether you found a bug, want a new feature, have a suggestion for an existing tool, or just want to share your thoughts, this is the place to send it. I read every message personally because I am a solo developer and every piece of feedback goes directly to me, not to a support team in another timezone.

The feedback form below is simple and fast. Choose the type of feedback from the dropdown, describe your experience in the message box, and send it directly to my inbox. You can leave your email if you would like a reply, or keep it blank if you prefer to stay anonymous. Bug reports with details like your device model and Android version usually help me fix issues faster.

I take every message seriously. If you report a crash, a layout problem, or something that does not work as expected, I will investigate it and try to fix it in the next release. If you suggest a feature, I will review it and check if it fits the direction of the app. If you just want to say something nice, that also matters — small messages keep me motivated to keep building every day.

Please do not send spam, advertising, or unrelated messages. This form exists for real users who want to help improve the apps, and I want to keep it clean and useful for everyone. Keep your message respectful and clear, and I will do my best to reply as soon as possible. Thank you for taking the time to help MARU grow and become better with every release.

Every bug report, every suggestion, and every kind word shapes the future of MARU. Some of the most useful features in my apps started as simple messages from users just like you. When you tell me something is broken, you are helping everyone who uses the app. When you suggest an improvement, you are guiding my roadmap. Your voice genuinely matters here, and I do not take it for granted.

I believe transparency builds trust. That is why I am open about what I can and cannot do. As a solo developer, I cannot respond to every message within minutes, and I cannot implement every idea that gets suggested. But I can promise that I will read everything, consider it honestly, and explain my decisions clearly. If a request is outside my current roadmap, I will tell you honestly.

To help me understand your feedback better, include as much context as you can. Which app were you using? What were you doing when the problem happened? Does the issue repeat every time, or only occasionally? These small details make an enormous difference when I am trying to reproduce a bug, and they often mean the difference between a fix that ships in days and one that takes weeks.

Thank you again for being part of this journey. MARU is small, independent, and built with care, and every message I receive reminds me why I keep building. I will read your feedback personally, think about it carefully, and use it to make the next version of every app just a little bit better. Your trust is the most important thing I have, and I will not break it.
